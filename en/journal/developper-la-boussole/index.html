Development log

Developing La Boussole around a booked seat

For an organiser, the available-seat counter must tell the same story as the household’s booking.

Show the journey to a group

The public demo allows role changes without registering a family. A household picks a ride, books two places and releases them. Trying this in one tab helps a group discuss the wording, the ride and when confirmation appears. Pilot preparation adds a human responsibility: deciding who opens the group and who investigates a surprising seat count.

Check what the organiser reads

In a local lab, two fictional accounts used the application’s server code with the Auth and Firestore emulators. On a four-seat ride, the organiser read two free places before booking, zero afterwards and two after cancellation. Each observation came from a fresh authenticated read. The pilot still needs observations on the group’s devices and network.
