Tracing sidepod flow from W13 to W16

A plan view becomes easier to read if you keep three reference points fixed: the front wheel, the inlet and the floor edge.

Start in Bahrain and Monaco

On the 2022 Bahrain test W13, find the narrow opening behind the front wheel. Much of the floor is exposed in plan view. The 2023 Monaco W14 occupies more of that space. Look at the upper front wishbone too. Mercedes changed a pickup alongside the bodywork. Attributing the difference to the sidepod alone would overlook that change.

Move on to the later cars

Align the front axles before switching from the Bahrain W15 to the Melbourne W16. Isolate the inlets, then restore the complete cars. The first view reveals the shape of the mouth. The second locates it relative to suspension and floor. Overlaying two reconstructions helps frame a question about shape. Quantifying any downforce change would require measurements.
