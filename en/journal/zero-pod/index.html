Reading the W13 zero-pod bodywork

On the Bahrain specification, tightly packaged bodywork leaves a particularly clear view of the upper floor edge.

Find the cooling inlet

Turn the car to a front three-quarter view. Between the wheel and the cockpit is a tall, narrow mouth. Isolate the sidepod cooling inlets, then make the surroundings translucent. Isolation shows the outline. Transparency locates it behind the wheel. The team retained cooling inlets on this specification.

Read the visible shape

The exterior width tells us about the space available for external flow. It cannot establish the position, inclination or mass flow of the heat exchangers. Monaco 2023 makes a useful comparison at the same orientation. Capture the floor edge in both views and find the same suspension endpoint before comparing the silhouettes.
