Development log

Modelling a car in Blender so that it can be explained

Object choices in the master file determine what a visitor will later be able to select.

Name what can be identified

A wheel contains a tyre, a rim and brakes. In the master, object names and collections distinguish assemblies even when several pieces share a black material. The workshop starts from those names. Public photographs help check the exterior shape. Hidden components remain a personal interpretation.

Preserve continuous surfaces

The W13 master has separate left and right ramp objects. On the three later cars the floor and ramp form two continuous half-surfaces. The workshop colours the faces on the rear upsweep and retains the original vertices. Isolating the region also reveals its chosen leading boundary. That boundary is a reading aid, not a manufacturing joint.
