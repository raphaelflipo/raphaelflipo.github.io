Making one GLB explorable without downloading a second car

A phone visitor can read the dossier before deciding whether to download the 3D scene.

Allocate the bytes

Text and a still render arrive first. Activation loads the GLB and an index tied to its SHA-256 hash. The six indexes in this version total 13,636 bytes before HTTP compression. They describe triangle membership independently of materials. The viewer can isolate a brake without carrying a separate copy of the car for every selection.

Measure the visitor’s action

A homepage score cannot tell us how long a scene takes to activate. Open a fresh page, check that no GLB was requested before the click, then measure through the first rendered selection. To judge motion after loading, observe rotation or zoom on the target hardware. A software renderer offers a reproducible comparison for changes to the site.
