Reading ground effect from beneath the car

Follow the passage between road and floor, from the inlet fences to the rear ramp.

Where the passage narrows

The road forms one boundary of the flow. At conserved mass flow and density, a smaller streamtube area implies a higher mean speed. Interpreting pressure over the entire floor also requires knowing how flow enters and leaves. Ride height in a fixed model represents a single position.

Where the passage opens

Switch from Floor to Diffuser ramp. On the W16 the colour marks the rising rear portions of two continuous surfaces. The selection’s leading edge is a reading aid. In the real flow, expansion can recover pressure if the boundary layer stays attached. The sketch illustrates that condition. It supplies neither downforce nor an optimum ride height.
