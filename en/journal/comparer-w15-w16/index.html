Three checks before comparing the W15 and W16

The W15 depicts Bahrain 2024 and the W16 Melbourne 2025. The models represent distinct race specifications.

Pick a common reference

Align the front axles and keep the viewing direction fixed. Isolate front suspension on both cars. Locate each link at the upright, then restore the bodywork to find its other end. An oblique view helps separate two links that overlap in profile.

Account for the weekend depicted

The studio W16 wears intermediates. The W15 wears slicks. Visible tread differences therefore also reflect the selected configurations. To compare cooling inlets, isolate them before looking at paint or tyres. The FIA submissions for each event supply context for the parts presented.
