Designing a sidepod around two air paths

On the Monaco W14, the cooling mouth and the undercut beneath the sidepod can be seen from the same viewpoint.

Follow the air entering the car

The lip defines the mouth of the duct. A frontal view helps locate it. A side view makes its projected depth easier to read. Only the opening can be checked from outside. Calculating mass flow would require pressure conditions and losses through the cooling circuit.

Follow the air around it

Return to the complete bodywork and follow the undercut towards the floor edge. Its upstream flow also depends on the tyre wake and suspension links. At Monaco the sidepod revision accompanied a front suspension update. Isolate them in turn to see their proximity.
