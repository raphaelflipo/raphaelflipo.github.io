La Boussole

Coordinate car seats as a group before a scout weekend.

Context
A scout weekend involves several families and drivers.
Problem
Scattered trip details make seats and cancellations hard to track.
Objective
Bring trips, requests and available seats together.
Method
Start with situations from the group, then test with fictional events and profiles.
My role
Design and develop the application and its public site.
Tools
C++, Qt, QML and Firebase.
Solution
A private carpooling app and a separate web demo.
Verifiable results
The public demo lets visitors try booking, cancelling and joining a waiting list. Version 1.0 is in private demonstration.
Skills
User needs analysis, cross-platform development and workflow validation.
