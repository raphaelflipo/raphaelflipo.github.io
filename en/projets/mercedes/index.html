Mercedes W13–W16

Recreate and compare four generations of Mercedes Formula 1 cars.

Context
The four models represent Grand Prix configurations from 2022 to 2025.
Problem
Individual renders give few references for understanding changes in shape.
Objective
Make it possible to inspect comparable areas with consistent views and accessible sources.
Method
Review photographs and technical presentations, model in Blender, then check the exports in a browser.
My role
Build the personal models, prepare the web files and create the presentation.
Tools
Blender, glTF/GLB, JavaScript, Three.js and WebGL.
Solution
A studio, individual viewers and a comparison tool with overlay and synchronised cameras.
Verifiable results
Four models and a comparison tool are available. The engineering notes distinguish public references from reconstructed geometry.
Skills
Surface modelling, reference analysis and web rendering experimentation.

The decision

On two three-quarter renders, perspective alone can make one sidepod look wider. I used a common viewpoint and two documented race specifications to compare the W15 and W16. To identify a component I trace it back to the published mesh.

What I verified

The Blender sources retain individual objects. A separate triangle index lets the browser use the original driver variants and livery.
