Engineering dossier

The Bahrain W15

Mercedes introduced a new chassis and gearbox casing in 2024. The cockpit moved rearwards and the rear suspension switched to pushrods. The gallery shows Lewis Hamilton’s No. 44 on the red-striped C3 soft tyres used at the start. Hamilton finished seventh at Sakhir, two places behind teammate George Russell in No. 63.

From the nose to the floor
