Motorsport engineering glossary

Bilingual definitions for the Mercedes W13 to W16 dossiers, from downforce to vorticity and suspension kinematics.

Back to the dossiers

Terms

Aerodynamic balance Balance aérodynamique

Distribution of downforce between axles. One convention uses front downforce divided by total downforce.

How much of the load reaches the front tyres?

W16 · Front wingW16 · Nose and front chassis

Aerodynamic efficiency Rendement aérodynamique

Ratio of downforce to drag under defined conditions and in a given configuration, often written |Cz|/Cx.

Downforce obtained for a given resistance.

Physical principles

Airbox Prise d’air supérieure

Intake above the cockpit which captures induction flow and may house auxiliary passages.

The opening above the driver.

W16 · Upper air intake

Anti-dive Anti-plongée

Portion of braking load routed geometrically through the links to resist front suspension compression.

The geometry helps keep the nose from diving under braking.

W16 · Front suspension

Anti-squat Anti-cabrage

Link geometry routes part of the acceleration load in a way that resists rear suspension compression.

The rear squats less under acceleration.

W16 · Rear suspension

Beam wing Aileron arrière inférieur

Lower set of elements downstream of the diffuser and below the main rear wing. It contributes to the rear pressure field.

The small wing near the diffuser.

W16 · Rear diffuserW16 · Engine cover and cooling

Boundary layer Couche limite

Region next to a wall where viscosity strongly alters the velocity. A transition can change the layer from laminar to turbulent.

The thin region where air is slowed by the surface.

W16 · Front wing

Centre of pressure Centre de pression

Equivalent application point of the pressure resultant under a specified force and moment convention. It varies with the car’s state.

The equivalent point where aerodynamic loading acts.

Physical principles

Chord Corde

Reference distance from the leading edge to the trailing edge at a specified section.

The local front-to-back length of a wing section.

Physical principles

Circulation Circulation

Line integral of the tangential velocity around a closed contour. It relates to section force under idealised conditions.

A measure of how the air moves around a wing section.

W16 · Rear wing and beam wing

Coke-bottle taper Pincement arrière

Narrowing of the bodywork around the gearbox and near the rear tyres, providing a passage for external airflow.

The bodywork tapers in before the rear axle.

W16 · Sidepods and undercutW16 · Engine cover and cooling

Diffuser Diffuseur

Terminal divergent part of the underbody where flow can regain some static pressure.

The ramp that opens up the air passage at the rear.

W16 · Rear diffuser

Downforce Déportance, ou appui

Component of aerodynamic force directed towards the track. With vertical upward defined positive, its lift coefficient is negative.

Air presses the car onto the track.

W16 · DRS and movable flap

Drag Traînée

Aerodynamic force component in the direction of incoming relative flow. In still air and straight-line motion, it opposes travel.

Air resistance to the car moving forward.

W16 · DRS and movable flap

Drag Reduction System (DRS) Réduction de traînée

Regulated rear-flap rotation system that changes wing-assembly circulation and drag.

The rear flap moves to make straight-line acceleration easier.

W16 · DRS and movable flap

Dynamic pressure Pression dynamique

Quantity q = ½ρV² in pascals, the kinetic energy per unit volume of flow with density ρ.

The pressure scale associated with airspeed.

Physical principles

Endplate Dérive latérale

Surface at a wing end that shapes three-dimensional junction flow and spanwise pressure leakage.

The wall at the tip of the wing.

W16 · Front wingW16 · Rear wing and beam wing

Flap Volet

Secondary element whose incidence, curvature and relative position affect turning and load of the assembly.

A wing element behind the mainplane.

W16 · Front wingW16 · Rear wing and beam wing

Floor fences Cloisons d’entrée du plancher

Streamwise surfaces at a shaped floor inlet that distribute and turn flow and can generate vorticity.

Air guides in front of the tunnels.

W16 · Floor and ground effect

Flow separation Décollement de l’écoulement

Breakaway of the boundary layer from a wall, often under an adverse pressure gradient, potentially with recirculation.

The air stops following the surface.

W16 · Wheels, tyres and brakesW16 · Sidepods and undercut

Ground effect Effet de sol

Change in aerodynamic force due to proximity to the ground, through altered flow conditions and pressure distribution.

The floor and the road act as a shared air passage.

W16 · Floor and ground effect

Halo Halo

Protective structure around the cockpit opening, subject to strength requirements.

The safety structure above the driver.

W16 · Halo, cockpit and mirrors

Induced drag Traînée induite

Part of drag associated with generating lift or downforce on a finite-span surface.

Part of the aerodynamic cost of producing downforce.

W16 · Rear wing and beam wing

Mainplane Profil principal

Primary load-carrying wing element, defined by section, camber, chord and spanwise position.

The wing’s main element, working in combination with the flaps.

W16 · Front wingW16 · Nose and front chassis

Mass flow rate Débit massique

Mass crossing a section per unit time. For uniform flow normal to the area, ṁ = ρVA.

How much air mass passes every second.

W16 · Wheels, tyres and brakesW16 · Sidepod cooling inlets

Pitch Tangage

Rotation about the transverse axis of the car, changing front and rear ride heights.

The nose rises or falls.

W16 · Front suspensionW16 · Rear suspension

Porpoising Marsouinage

Vertical or pitch oscillation sustained by coupling between aerodynamic loading and chassis dynamics.

Changing downforce keeps the body moving up and down.

W16 · Floor and ground effect

Pressure coefficient Coefficient de pression

Local static pressure minus reference pressure, divided by reference dynamic pressure. Cp = (p − p∞)/q∞.

A way to compare pressure regions on a common scale.

W16 · Nose and front chassis

Pressure gradient Gradient de pression

Spatial variation of pressure. An adverse gradient means pressure rises in the flow direction and decelerates the boundary layer.

Rising pressure can cause the flow near the surface to separate.

W16 · Rear diffuser

Pressure recovery Récupération de pression

Rise in static pressure as flow decelerates in an expansion. With losses, total pressure is not recovered.

The diffuser slows the air while raising its static pressure.

W16 · Sidepod cooling inletsW16 · Rear diffuser

Pull-rod Tirant

Link that chiefly transfers tension to the inboard suspension when the wheel moves into bump.

The wheel pulls on the inboard mechanism.

W16 · Front suspensionW16 · Rear suspension

Push-rod Poussoir

Link that chiefly transfers compression to the inboard suspension when the wheel moves into bump.

The wheel pushes on the inboard mechanism.

W16 · Front suspensionW16 · Rear suspension

Reynolds number Nombre de Reynolds

Dimensionless ratio of inertial to viscous effects. Re = ρVL/μ for reference length L.

A scale indicator used to compare flows.

Physical principles

Ride height Hauteur de caisse

Distance from a specified car reference point to the road. Its value depends on that point, load and speed.

The gap between the car and the road.

W16 · Front suspensionW16 · Floor and ground effect

Roll Roulis

Rotation about the longitudinal axis, changing left and right ride heights.

The car leans as it can in a corner.

W16 · Floor and ground effect

Sidepod Ponton

Lateral bodywork that packages the cooling inlet and heat exchangers while shaping external flow.

The bodywork on either side of the cockpit.

W16 · Sidepods and undercutW16 · Sidepod cooling inlets

Slot gap Fente interprofils

Passage between wing elements. It changes inflow and pressure recovery on the downstream element.

The opening between two wing elements.

W16 · Front wingW16 · DRS and movable flap

Static pressure Pression statique

Local thermodynamic pressure acting in all directions at a point in the fluid.

The pressure air exerts on a surface.

Physical principles

Total pressure Pression totale

Pressure obtained by bringing flow to rest without loss. In incompressible flow, p₀ = p + q. It falls in a dissipative wake.

An indicator of the mechanical energy available in the air.

W16 · Wheels, tyres and brakesW16 · Sidepod cooling inlets

Undercut Creusement sous ponton

Recess below the sidepod shoulder and above the floor that shapes the streamwise air passage.

The hollow under the front and side of the sidepod.

W16 · Sidepods and undercut

Venturi tunnel Tunnel Venturi

Converging then diverging passage with a minimum-area region. A racing underfloor remains three-dimensional and open at the sides.

Air crosses a narrow part before the passage opens out.

W16 · Floor and ground effect

Vortex Tourbillon

Structure where velocity winds around a local axis. Vorticity is the curl of the velocity field.

A rotating structure in the flow.

W16 · Floor and ground effect

Wake Sillage

Region downstream of a body where velocity, total pressure and turbulence differ from the incoming flow.

Disturbed air behind a tyre or suspension link.

W16 · Wheels, tyres and brakesW16 · Sidepods and undercut

Yaw Lacet

Rotation about the vertical axis. Relative aerodynamic yaw describes the misalignment between the car and incoming flow.

Air arrives slightly from the side.
