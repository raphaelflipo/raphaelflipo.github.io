Modelling a Formula 1 rear wing in 3D

In my final year of secondary school, I modelled an F1 rear wing for an engineering science project. Rotate the assembly to see how the wing profiles join the two endplates.

Rear wing preview. The GLB file also opens in a 3D viewer.

I converted the STEP CAD file to GLB so the assembly could be viewed in a browser.

Why an F1 rear wing?

I have followed motorsport for years. I chose a part you can recognise on a car from a distance. Up close, you can see how several separate shapes make up the wing’s outline.

What to look for

The front view shows the stacked wing profiles. Rotate the assembly a quarter turn to see the cutouts and openings in the endplates.

What I did next

After this school project, I started developing applications. I created La Boussole to organise carpooling for my scout troop.
