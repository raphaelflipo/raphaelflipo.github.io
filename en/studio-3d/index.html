How the design evolved, 2022–2025

Open a season to see documented changes on the real car.

  1. 2022W13 · Narrow sidepods arrive

    In Bahrain, Mercedes unveiled much narrower sidepod inlets than on the version tested in Barcelona.

    Formula 1 analysis

  2. 2023W14 · A change of direction in Monaco

    The W14 gained wider, higher sidepod inlets. Mercedes also revised the front suspension and the fences at the leading edge of the floor.

    FIA Monaco submission

  3. 2024W15 · New chassis and sidepods

    Mercedes introduced a new chassis and gearbox casing. The cockpit moved rearwards, the sidepods were reshaped and the rear suspension switched to pushrods.

    Mercedes launch notes Formula 1 analysis

  4. 2025W16 · Two inlet orientations

    In Melbourne, Mercedes documented horizontal and vertical inlets, a deeper sidepod undercut and a lower-set steering track rod on the front suspension.

    FIA Australian submission
