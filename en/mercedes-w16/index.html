Engineering dossier

The W16 in Melbourne

Mercedes revised airflow along the floor with horizontal and vertical sidepod intakes and a deeper undercut. The front suspension geometry changed, while pushrods remained at both axles.

George Russell finished third and Andrea Kimi Antonelli fourth. Both liveries feature the green-striped intermediates used at the Melbourne start. The W15’s slick tyres match the dry start at Bahrain.

From the W15 to the W16

The nose meets the mainplane

In Bahrain 2024, the slot runs beneath the W15’s nose. On the Melbourne W16, the nose meets the front wing mainplane and interrupts the slot at the centre.

Two intake orientations

The triangular opening seen on the Bahrain W15 gives way to two intake areas, one horizontal beneath the lip and one vertical by the chassis.

A deeper sidepod undercut

The lower sidepod undercut extends further along the car. It is especially clear in the side and front three-quarter views with a grey finish.

A lower steering-link attachment

The steering link meets the chassis lower on the W16. The suspension arms, pushrod and steering link are visible behind the front wheel.
