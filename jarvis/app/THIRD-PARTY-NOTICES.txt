4. MODEL FILES DOWNLOADED SEPARATELY

Model weights and tokenizer assets are not included in the Jarvis Local ZIP.
The application downloads selected, pinned model revisions from Hugging Face.
Their licensing, provenance and model documentation are published separately:

  https://huggingface.co/HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-135M-Instruct
  https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/Qwen3-0.6B-ONNX
  Original Qwen model: https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-0.6B

The exact download revisions are recorded in models.js. Consult the model's
own license before separately redistributing its files or publishing changes.
